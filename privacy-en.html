Privacy Policy

Effective: September 2, 2026 · Version 1.1

This policy applies to mobile applications (“Apps”) published by devpyo (“Developer”). Features and SDKs vary by App; sections on analytics and advertising apply only to Apps that include those SDKs.

1. Contact

  • Email: dev.pyo@gmail.com

2. Data collected and purposes

Most Apps have no user accounts and do not store user-entered content on Developer servers. For Apps that include analytics or advertising SDKs, the following may be processed by the App and third parties:

CategoryExamplesProcessorPurpose
Usage data Feature events (e.g., item created, settings changed) Google Firebase Analytics Analytics, quality improvement
Advertising data Ad impressions, clicks, device ad signals Google AdMob Banner ads, ad measurement
Device data OS, app version, language, device model Google, Apple Compatibility, service delivery
Network data IP address (as needed for ads / SDK operation) Google AdMob, etc. Ad delivery, fraud prevention
Diagnostics / performance SDK / ad load performance and error signals Google SDK stability and performance
User content Text, dates entered in the App User device (and optional iCloud) Core app features

Apps with analytics or ads do not use IDFA and do not show the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. Firebase Analytics uses a configuration without ad identifiers (FirebaseAnalyticsWithoutAdIdSupport).

Apps with ads may use Apple SKAdNetwork for aggregated ad performance measurement. This is not used to identify you personally and is separate from IDFA.

IP addresses and diagnostics / performance data may be processed by Google AdMob and other third-party SDKs for ad delivery and SDK operation. Developer does not collect or use IDFA.

3. What we do not collect

  • Account credentials (name, email, phone) when no sign-in exists
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, camera, precise location
  • Upload of user content to Developer servers
  • IDFA or cross-app tracking identifiers
  • Direct crash reporting via Firebase Crashlytics or similar (Apps without those SDKs)

4. Storage and sync

  • App data is stored on the user’s device.
  • Some Apps may sync via Apple iCloud when enabled. Developer cannot access that data.
  • Widgets and local notifications use on-device data only.

6. Retention

Retention periods under applicable law include:

  • On-device / iCloud data: until deleted by the user or app uninstall (or iCloud sync is disabled)
  • Analytics / ads data: per Google service policies (e.g., Analytics event retention per console settings)
  • Support email: deleted within a reasonable time after resolution (typically within one year)

7. Your rights

Depending on your region (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, Korea PIPA, etc.), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict processing, or withdraw consent. Contact: dev.pyo@gmail.com

Users in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland may see AdMob UMP consent UI and may change ad preferences per UMP policy and in-app options where provided.

8. Children

Apps are not directed at children under 13 (or local age of consent). We do not knowingly collect their data.

9. International transfers

Data may be processed outside your country (e.g., the United States) by Google and Apple. Google may use applicable transfer mechanisms such as the Data Privacy Framework where relevant.

10. Security

Developer uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We recommend not entering sensitive information in the App.
