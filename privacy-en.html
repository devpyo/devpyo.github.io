Privacy Policy

Effective: September 2, 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy applies to mobile applications (“Apps”) published by devpyo (“Developer”). App-specific details are in the Appendix.

1. Contact

  • Email: dev.pyo@gmail.com
  • GitHub: github.com/devpyo

2. Information we process

Most Apps have no user accounts and do not store user-entered content on Developer servers. The following may still be processed by the App and third-party SDKs:

CategoryExamplesProcessorPurpose
Usage data Feature events (e.g., item created, settings changed) Google Firebase Analytics Analytics, quality improvement
Advertising data Ad impressions, clicks, device ad signals Google AdMob Banner ads
Device data OS, app version, language, device model Google, Apple Compatibility, service delivery
User content Text, dates entered in the App User device (and optional iCloud) Core app features

Current Apps do not use IDFA and do not show the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. Firebase Analytics runs without ad identifiers.

3. What we do not collect

  • Account credentials (name, email, phone) when no sign-in exists
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, camera, precise location
  • Upload of user content to Developer servers
  • IDFA or cross-app tracking identifiers

4. Storage and sync

  • App data is stored on the user’s device.
  • Some Apps may sync via Apple iCloud when enabled. Developer cannot access that data.
  • Widgets and notifications use on-device data only.

6. Retention

  • On-device / iCloud data: until deleted by the user or app uninstall
  • Analytics / ads data: per Google service policies
  • Support email: deleted within a reasonable time after resolution

7. Your rights

Depending on your region (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, Korea PIPA, etc.), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing. Contact: dev.pyo@gmail.com

Users in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland may see AdMob UMP consent UI.

8. Children

Apps are not directed at children under 13 (or local age of consent). We do not knowingly collect their data.

9. International transfers

Data may be processed outside your country (e.g., the United States) by Google and Apple.

10. Changes

We update the effective date and version on this page. Material changes may be noted in app release notes.
