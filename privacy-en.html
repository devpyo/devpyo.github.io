Privacy Policy

Effective: September 2, 2026 · Last updated: September 2, 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy applies to mobile applications (“Apps”) published by devpyo (“Developer”). If an App differs from this policy, the App appendix controls for that App.

Drafted with reference to Apple App Store privacy requirements, Firebase/Google Analytics disclosures, and Google AdMob / UMP practices. Not legal advice.

1. Contact

  • Developer: devpyo
  • Email: dev.pyo@gmail.com
  • GitHub: https://github.com/devpyo/

2. Scope

This policy covers iOS Apps distributed by the Developer (e.g., on the Apple App Store). Third-party SDKs may have their own policies.

3. General principles

  • No accounts in most Apps (unless noted in the appendix).
  • On-device first: user-entered content is stored on the device by default.
  • No developer-server storage of user content unless stated in the appendix.
  • No IDFA / no ATT prompt in current Apps.
  • Analytics: Firebase Analytics may be used with FirebaseAnalyticsWithoutAdIdSupport.
  • Ads: some Apps may show Google AdMob banner ads.

5. What we do not collect (by default)

  • Account credentials (name, email, phone) when no sign-in exists
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, camera, precise location
  • IDFA or cross-app tracking identifiers
  • Upload of user content to Developer-operated servers

6. Storage, sync, permissions

  • Data is stored in the app sandbox or App Group on device.
  • Some Apps may sync via Apple iCloud Documents when enabled; Developer does not access that data.
  • Widgets read on-device data via App Group.
  • Local notifications are scheduled on device only.

Developer does not sell personal information (CCPA/CPRA sense).

8. Advertising consent (UMP)

Apps with ads may use Google UMP to collect consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland. Other regions may receive ads per app configuration.

9. Retention

  • On-device / iCloud data: until deleted by the user or app uninstall.
  • Analytics / ads data: per Google service policies.
  • Support email: deleted within a reasonable time after resolution.

10. Your rights

Depending on your region (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, Korea PIPA, etc.), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing. Contact dev.pyo@gmail.com.

11. Children

Apps are not directed at children under 13 (or local age of consent). We do not knowingly collect their data.

12. International transfers

Data may be processed in the United States and other countries by Google and Apple under their applicable transfer mechanisms.

13. Security

We use reasonable safeguards. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

14. Changes

We may update this page and change the “Last updated” date. Material changes may be communicated via app release notes.
